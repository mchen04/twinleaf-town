Reference and verification limits

The upper screen scrolls over the world. Both screen surfaces use the 256:192 DS aspect ratio at 2× rendering size. The ordinary no-Poketch lower background now uses original indexed tile, screen-map and palette inputs with native 2D color and brightness conversion, rather than the compressed-video crop. Generated NCGR byte equivalence and original display parity are unverified. Dialogue font, frame and continuation-arrow pixels now derive from original source assets. Eight unconditional human actors use source-authored dialogue controls in an explicit early-branch demo state; conditional story actors are excluded. Mock-helper pagination and added choice layout are adaptations. Selected door/pond/TV source animations, door camera phases and integer fades are now integrated, but display phase and exact visual parity are unverified.

Music is derived from the supplied original Twinleaf Town (Day) reference. Its numerical loop candidate has not been audibly verified. The player and four Pokémon use decoded original sprite frames. Scenes now render native source meshes with decoded outdoor perspective and indoor orthographic cameras. Source billboard geometry, projection-depth bias and event transforms are applied. The app uses an explicit integer native-polygon path with verified lighting tables at a fixed-time preview (noon by default). Source-byte gates admit the supported actors, exact door/camera samples and pond/TV states. Integer diffuse/specular RGB5 lighting follows the supplied pinned melonDS reference. Source task timing, browser performance and original hardware/display parity remain unverified. Added helper render positions are rounded to FX12 without changing their simulation or collision state. Animation timing and collision interpretation still need validation. Sign/mailbox models now use source-verified event resources. Audio enables original reference music and source-derived effects in the recording graph only. The graph has no connection to live speakers. Effect hardware parity and trigger timing are unverified.

Recording is development-only and user-initiated. It captures this canvas and this app’s actual audio graph to WebM where supported, with no microphone, external mux, upload or permission change. Compatibility and recorded playback require real-browser checks.
